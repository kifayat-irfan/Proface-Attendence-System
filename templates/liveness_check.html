How to verify the anti-spoofing CNN

  1. Open Live Marking in another tab.
  2. Test 1 — real face: stand before the camera. Your box should be green "Live" with a CNN score above 0.65.
  3. Test 2 — printed photo: hold a printed photo (or phone screen) of a registered student to the camera. The box should turn red "Fake - not marked" and attendance must NOT be marked.
  4. Test 3 — blink: blink naturally; the EAR detector confirms liveness even if the CNN is unsure.

⚙️ If the results look inverted

The CNN was trained with alphabetical class folders (fake=0, real=1). If real faces score as "Fake" and photos score as "Live", the class mapping is flipped on your model copy — fix it with one line in app.py:

LIVENESS_REAL_INDEX = 0 # was 1

Then restart the app and re-run the tests above.
